Safety & Tips

Small habits keep you safe. Open a topic to read more.

Room security
  • Use a strong padlock or door lock; never lend your key.
  • Keep windows locked at night.
  • Know where the fire exit is.
Getting home safely
  • Avoid walking alone late; use a trusted boda or taxi and share the number plate.
  • Charge your phone before going out.
Valuables
  • Keep laptops and phones out of sight near windows.
  • Mark your devices and note serial numbers.
Fire and electricity
  • Do not overload sockets or leave cookers on.
  • Report broken wiring to the manager in writing.
Mobile money safety
  • Never share your PIN, even with someone claiming to be from the network.
  • Check the name on screen before you confirm.
