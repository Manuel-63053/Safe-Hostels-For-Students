Help & Contact

Already sent money to someone suspicious?

  1. Stop paying. Send nothing more.
  2. Keep evidence: messages, numbers, receipts and mobile money SMS.
  3. Contact your mobile money provider quickly and report the transaction.
  4. Report to the police and tell the Dean of Students.

In danger? Call Uganda Police emergency: 999 or 112.
